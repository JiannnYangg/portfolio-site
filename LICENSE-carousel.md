# Third-party code: liquid-glass-carousel

`app/_layout/showcase/engine.js` and `app/_layout/showcase/config.js` are based
on [liquid-glass-carousel](https://github.com/Yousuf-developer/liquid-glass-carousel)
by Yousuf Soomro, used under the MIT License. They have been modified for
embedding in this site (transparent background, page-scroll friendly input,
pause/resume, cleanup); the changes are listed at the top of `engine.js`.

The demo images from that repository are **not** included: they are third-party
work that its CREDITS.md says must not be reused. The panels in
`public/showcase/` are original placeholders.
